Case Study — Cyber Threat Incident

🌾 Food & Agriculture · USA / Canada / Australia

JBS Foods Ransomware Attack (May 2021)

Attacker: REvil / Sodinokibi ransomware-as-a-service group
Nature of the Threat

On 30 May 2021, REvil ransomware encrypted IT systems supporting plant scheduling, ERP and production-control functions at JBS – the world's largest meat processor – across its US, Canadian and Australian operations.

Impact

Thirteen major US meatpacking plants were idled, along with facilities in Canada and Australia, halting roughly 20% of US beef-processing capacity for about one day. JBS confirmed paying an $11 million ransom in Bitcoin to prevent further disruption.

Key Security Lessons
  • IT/OT segmentation matters: JBS pre-emptively shut down production-control systems to stop the IT-side infection reaching OT, showing the value of network isolation – but also the cost of weak separation
  • Offline/immutable backups allowed a faster recovery and reduced ransom leverage
  • A single ransomware incident at one global processor can disrupt national food-supply chains across multiple countries simultaneously
Source: FBI Press Release, Jun. 2021
